Privacy Policy

At KC97 Academy, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by KC97 Academy and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register to take a mock test, we may ask for your contact information, including items such as name, email address, phone number, and location. This data is collected strictly with your consent to generate personalized scorecards and maintain academic records.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and test portal.
  • Generate and display your exam results and subject-wise analytics.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our educational content.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

Our mock test results are processed using secure third-party services like Google Sheets API purely for database management. We do not sell or share your personal data with marketing agencies.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
